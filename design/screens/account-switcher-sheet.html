Entry point: Settings ▸ Switch account (also long-press on the identity row). Sheet lists every saved account grouped by server; the active account carries the white “Current” pill (selected-pill vocabulary). Switching is sign-out + sign-in under the hood, so the tapped row shows an inline spinner while every other control disables (frame 2); success dismisses the sheet into the post-switch Home moment (screens/switch-edge-cases.html). If the held token turns out expired (frame 3), the row flips to the error-badged needs-sign-in state with an inline “Sign in” pill that opens Login prefilled — the user stays signed in as Sam until re-auth succeeds. “Add another server” leaves to Server setup. The downloads footnote appears only here (the one place users wonder). On tablets the same content renders as a centered modal at 480dp.
