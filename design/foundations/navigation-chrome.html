Navigation chrome

Every screen has exactly one way out of it, in the same place. Back is that control. Home is a destination in the bottom nav or rail, not a header button — putting both in a header gives two adjacent controls that look like the same affordance and do different things.

Rule 01
Back is the leading control, or there is none
36dp glass icon button, first child of the header, 8dp before the title. Top-level tabs (Home, Libraries, Search, Downloads) draw no back button at all — the nav bar is their context.
Rule 02
Home never appears in a header
Home is reachable from the bottom nav pill on compact and the rail on wide. A header Home button duplicates a control that is already on screen.
Rule 03
Immersive screens are the one exception
Detail and Player hide the nav bar, and Detail can be entered by deep link with no back stack. Detail carries a Home button at the trailing edge of its overlay row, never beside Back.
Rule 04
The trailing edge is for screen actions
Search, filter, cast, favourite, overflow. Navigation never sits on the right of a header; actions never sit on the left.
